More than just PvP

Explore the Minecraft universe

Minecraft is a sandbox game where players explore, build, craft, survive and create their own adventures. Here's a quick map of what the game offers.

What is Minecraft?

Minecraft is a sandbox game built around blocks and creativity. Players can gather resources, craft tools, build structures, explore different worlds and play with friends. The experience changes depending on the edition, game mode, server and player-created content.

Two popular editions

Java Edition: commonly played on Windows, macOS and Linux, with a large mod and server community.

Bedrock Edition: available across supported consoles, phones, tablets and Windows, with cross-platform multiplayer on supported devices.

PvP fundamentals

Build better fundamentals

Good PvP starts with movement, awareness, timing and calm decision-making.

Open learning checklist

Practise movement in a safe training world, learn your chosen mode's rules, review your matches, and focus on one skill at a time. Use fair-play settings and avoid cheats.

Game knowledge

Know your equipment

Understand your edition, game version, items and the rules of the server you join.

Open learning checklist

Check Java or Bedrock, confirm the server version, read its rules, and learn what each item does in that specific game mode.

Community safety

Play smart online

Choose well-moderated communities and keep personal information private.

Open safety checklist

Never share passwords, home address, school details or private payment information. Ask a parent or guardian before joining unfamiliar communities or arranging paid sessions.
